Start messaging

Hermes Route is a private chat for two people. Messages travel straight from one browser to the other, with no account and nothing to install.

How it works

  1. Start a chat

    You get a private invite link. There is no sign-up and no password.

  2. Share the link

    Send it to one person. The link only works for the two of you, in the first two browsers that open it.

  3. Talk directly

    Your two browsers connect to each other and encrypt every message between them.

Our server only introduces the two browsers to each other. When two networks won't allow a direct connection, messages pass through our relay instead, still encrypted so that it can't read them. Nothing is saved anywhere: close the page and the conversation is gone.
